Hi, I'm so glad you're here

Homeschool tools, made by a homeschool mom.

I'm a homeschooling mom, and along the way I kept building little tools to make our own school days calmer and more organized. Friends started asking for them — so I decided to share what I've made with other homeschool families who might find them helpful too.

Right now that means desktop software and mobile apps, with curriculum hopefully coming somewhere down the road. It's a small but growing library of tools, and I add to it as I build and refine each new idea.

Everything here is made by a real family, for real families — no gimmicks, no clutter. Just simple, thoughtful tools to help homeschooling feel a little lighter. And because I know what homeschooling on a family budget looks like, I price everything with homeschool families in mind — these tools are meant to be genuinely affordable.
