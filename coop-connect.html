Coop Connect

Your homeschool co-op, organized in one calm place. A shared calendar with one-tap RSVPs, sign-up sheets for volunteers and potlucks, announcements nobody misses, and lesson plans that reschedule themselves when a week is missed. Children are never named — the app keeps only a headcount.

Free to join

Members always join free. Leaders who run a co-op subscribe to Co-op Ownership: $4.99 a month for your first co-op and $4 for each one after, with unlimited members. Billed through Google Play; cancel anytime in your store settings.
