OatSmart stores all of your homeschool records — student profiles, pictures of your students, grades, assignments, attendance, calendars, and reports — in a local database on your computer. Where data lives
All app data is stored in a SQLite database in your system's application data folder. On macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/OatSmart/. On Windows: %APPDATA%/OatSmart/. On Linux: ~/.config/OatSmart/
Student pictures
Photos you attach to student profiles or assignments are stored on your computer with the rest of your data. They are never uploaded or transmitted to us.
Premium activities
Records of Premium games, lessons, and worksheets — including completed activities that convert into assignments and roll into your reports — are stored locally with the rest of your data, not on our servers.
License verification
The only outbound network call OatSmart makes is a one-time license check when you enter your subscription key. After verification, the app operates fully offline. No student data is transmitted during this check.
Backups & exported files
We strongly recommend backing up your OatSmart data folder regularly using your operating system's backup tools or an external drive; the app includes a built-in export function. Report cards, transcripts, and worksheets exported as PDFs are saved wherever you choose on your computer and contain no tracking or telemetry of any kind.
Uninstalling
Uninstalling OatSmart does not delete your data folder. You retain full ownership of all your records. To fully remove all data, delete the app data folder manually after uninstalling.
Our commitment
We will never add cloud sync, telemetry, or any data transmission to OatSmart without explicit opt-in consent and prior notice. Your family's records are yours — full stop.
