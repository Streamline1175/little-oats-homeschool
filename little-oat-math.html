Little Oat Math

Math practice for homeschool families, from Pre-K counting to 6th-grade ratios and equations — seven levels, more than 90 decks and 3,600 cards. Your child answers aloud, listens and chooses, types the answer, picks from multiple choice, or works a hands-on activity, cards come back just before they’d be forgotten until they’re mastered, and each student’s progress is there when you want it. No account, no ads, and everything stays on your phone.

Counting to ratios and equations

Seven levels: counting, numbers to 10, shapes and patterns; numbers to 100, ten frames, addition and subtraction facts, place value and time; carrying and borrowing, money, measuring and first fractions; multiplication, division, rounding, graphs, area and perimeter; long multiplication and division, decimals, lines and angles; order of operations, unit conversion, volume and the coordinate plane; and ratios, percentages, negative numbers, expressions and equations, and statistics. Make decks of your own under Custom, too.

A page for each student

Add each child with their own animal picture and progress. Each student’s page opens on a dashboard: lessons, practice time, cards mastered and cards still being learned — tap Lessons for every lesson, what it covered and exactly which cards were missed. Set up a lesson as a random set or pick the exact cards. A card is mastered after four correct answers in a row, spread over about a week, and you can mark a card or deck mastered yourself.

Logs straight into the Daily Tracker

Send a finished lesson to the Daily Work Tracker and it’s logged as graded daily work, with the topic and cards already filled in. Or turn on logging in Settings and lessons go in by themselves. It goes from one app to the other on your phone.

Stays on your phone

There’s no account, and everything stays on your device. Backup & export writes a backup file you save to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files or email, and restore on a new phone — backups are always free. You can also export progress as a spreadsheet. Seven warm color themes, each in light and dark.

Grown-ups only

Purchases, backups, the Daily Tracker settings, sending a lesson to the Daily Tracker, and adding or editing students sit behind a grown-ups-only question — a two-digit sum with a carry, typed in full — so little hands stay on the practice.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and your students and their progress are never sent to us. Online, the app checks only whether a subscription is active (through RevenueCat) and whether an app update is ready (through Expo). Cards are read aloud by two recorded voices, Wren and Miles: pick one when you start and change it any time. On iPhone both are built into the app and work offline; on Android a few recordings are built in and Google Play delivers the rest of the voice you choose, with your phone’s offline voice filling in until then. The app never uses the microphone.
