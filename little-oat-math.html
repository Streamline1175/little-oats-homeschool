Little Oat Math

Math flashcards for homeschool families, Pre-K through 5th grade — 84 decks and 1,195 cards, from counting to five to order of operations. Your child answers aloud, listens and chooses, types the answer, or races a timed drill, cards come back at the right time until they’re mastered, and each student’s progress is there when you want it. No account, no ads, and everything stays on your phone.

Counting to order of operations

Counting, numbers to 120, quick-look dot patterns, shapes, ten frames and teen numbers, addition and subtraction facts, make 10, doubles, place value, comparing numbers, telling time, coins, skip counting, even and odd, every times table and its division facts, fractions, rounding, area and volume, angles, prime and composite numbers, decimals, powers of ten and order of operations.

A page for each student

Add each child with their own grade and progress. Each student’s page opens on a dashboard: progress through their grade, lessons completed, cards mastered and cards still being learned — tap Lessons for every lesson, what it covered and exactly which cards were missed. Set up a lesson as a random set of any size or pick the exact cards, and mark any card mastered or new by hand.

Logs straight into the Daily Tracker

Send a finished lesson to the Daily Work Tracker and it’s logged as graded daily work, filed under Math with the topic, cards covered and your notes already filled in. It goes from one app to the other on your phone.

Stays on your phone

There’s no account, and everything stays on your device. Backup & export writes a backup file you save to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files or email, and restore on a new phone — backups are always free. You can also export CSV files of progress by deck and of every answer.

Grown-ups only

Settings, purchases, backups, and adding or editing students sit behind a two-digit multiplication question — harder than anything in the decks — so little hands stay on the flashcards.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only online service is RevenueCat, which checks whether a subscription is active. Cards are read aloud from recordings built into the app, in four voice styles — Natural, Calm, Excited and Robot — and the app never uses the microphone.
